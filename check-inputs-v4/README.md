# Inputs of the checks of version 4

Concrete inputs of the checks of version 4 (`fourth-paper/paper4-en.md`, Section 9). Section 9.1 keeps the
rule of the third paper: before the first observation of a check, its concrete inputs are committed to
the repository of check records. `PROTOCOL.md` was committed first; the files below were committed
before the first observation of checks 1 and 4 and before the first inventory of the machines examined
beyond the plan. The inputs of checks 2 and 3 are committed separately before their first observation.

- `inputs-check-1.md` — check 1: releases, choices of this check, clean installations, ordinary actions
  with their exact commands, the inventory collector and the facts it records.
- `inputs-check-4.md` — check 4: releases, clean installations, the facts to be established for each
  property.
- `inputs-beyond-plan.md` — the lived-in machines examined beyond the plan (`PROTOCOL.md`, Section 5).
- `DISCLOSURES.md` — what became known before the first observation.
- `input-files.sha256.tsv` — the files these inputs name, with their checksums.
- Named in the checksum list but not published here: `collector/probe-linux-amd64.gz.part-000` (not a text file); `collector/probe-linux-amd64.gz.part-001` (not a text file); `collector/probe-linux-amd64.gz.part-002` (not a text file); `collector/probe-linux-amd64.gz.part-003` (not a text file); `collector/probe-linux-amd64.gz.part-004` (not a text file).
