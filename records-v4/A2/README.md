# A2 — text against itself

This is the investigation beyond the published plan identified in PROTOCOL.md Section 5. It is separate from all four published checks and assigns no weight to a candidate. No virtual machine, container, installation, configuration change or system probe was run.

The three sweeps are recorded in PAIRS.tsv (741 pairs), TERMS.tsv (59 boundaries) and PROPERTIES.tsv (28 examinations). CARDS.md is the final filtered result: CARDS: 0. REVIEW.md records the independent adversarial review. Initial candidate notes in raw/ are retained only as search history and are not accepted findings. NAMES.tsv contains four disputes that do not change the required declaration.

PAIR_SENTENCES.tsv fixes the pair universe: all 23 sentences in Section 8.2 and all 16 definitions/supporting sentences in Section 8.1, including scope, priority and exemption sentences rather than silently dropping them. This conservative universe contains 39 sentences and exactly 39 × 38 / 2 = 741 unordered pairs. Each row records its tried object, the effect of each sentence and its disposition. “Consistent” means no opposite prescription survived on the stated witness; it is not a universal theorem about every possible machine. “Settled by the order” means an earlier affirmative question defeats a later overlapping predicate. A term boundary is not automatically a pairwise collision.

PROPERTIES.tsv begins with all 15 unordered pairs of P1, P2, P4, Understanding, Reproducibility and Removal, then examines grouped requirements and qualifiers. Local IDs for the six sentences of Section 8.4 are mapped without changing the published text in consequence_ids.md. TERMS.tsv distinguishes lack of a universal term definition from inability to place its concrete boundary object; a “no” does not become a card without the declaration/property difference required by the filter.

Documented objects are considered in stated conditions. This is a source-and-reasoning audit, not a claimed inventory of an observed machine. Unknown provenance, effective values, permissions or interpreter availability would be FACT_UNKNOWN in an inventory; those facts were not guessed. The coverage of installation/configuration channels was not investigated.

Primary URLs and short exact passages are indexed in PAIR_SOURCES.md, TERM_SOURCES.md, property_sources.md and ADDITIONAL_SOURCES.md. The scripts preserve enumeration and the final table dispositions. Validation output is in raw/validation.txt and raw/check_quotes.txt.

The search rejected ordinary implementation noncompliance, matters explicitly excluded by Section 8.5, and apparently different readings that give the same complete declaration. No examined candidate passed the full filter; this does not establish that the text has no gap. The required CARDS.md quotation check passed; the 39 sentence-inventory quotations and six consequence quotations also matched the published text.
