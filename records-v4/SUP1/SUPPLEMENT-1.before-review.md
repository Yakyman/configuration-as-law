# Supplement 1 to the order of the checks of version 4

Published on 23 November 2026, after the first night of checks and before the work on any step named here begins. It
does not change `PROTOCOL.md`.

The first night showed that part of the claims of the hypothesis is tested by none of the four checks of the plan.
This supplement does not change the outcomes of those four checks. The text of version 4 is not changed either: a
confirmed gap means that version 4 did not hold, and a correction will be a new version.

1. **Whether the consequences follow from the properties.** By reasoning, we look for two different states of a
   computer under one declaration, in each of which the three properties hold. The first case: what a package creates
   by itself during installation. Against the hypothesis: such states are found.
2. **Disputed cases with complete facts.** For each family of disagreements, all the facts are collected; two parties
   decide separately, a third adjudicates. In addition, a sample of the objects on which the parties agreed. Against: a
   fully known object receives two decisions or none.
3. **“At any moment” and a list from outside.** We write down what must be true at each point of an ordinary update,
   and what is in force for a list taken from a server when there is no network. Against: a moment for which the text
   does not determine what must be.
4. **The facts missing after the first night**, including Gentoo. New observations receive a new name; the earlier
   attempts remain as they were.
5. **One system in which the properties and ordinary work hold together:** a command shell, one's own recorded script,
   packages, a network, a browser, documents. Against: an ordinary action is impossible for a reason derived from the
   definitions. An unfinished testbed gives “?”.
6. **Reproducibility and removal**, with additions: installation in a different order, removal of the last owner of a
   shared object.
7. **A check of the checks themselves:** the way of telling a real gap from a far-fetched one is tried on cases known
   to be right and cases known to be wrong.
8. **Wider and longer:** updates, development, games, loss of network.

Rules for all steps: the inputs and the expected result are recorded before the first observation; each case is
attempted once; the outcome is 1, 0 or “?”; the work of a performer is checked by another party; gaps in the wording
and gaps in the idea are reported in two separate lists.
