Adjudication dated 2026-11-23 UTC. The c1 labels C1/C2/C4 correspond to P1/P2/P4. The account witnesses above are sufficient for P1 and P4 in each assessment; the additional package-installation arguments are not needed for these outcomes. Neither assessment gives a different decision or an `undecidable` decision in any of the six cases.

The account changes concern settings, not unchanged package contents or executable code. S8.2-13: “Settings are services and their parameters, the network, including saved networks, devices and filesystems, time and language, accounts, rights, access rules and what is permitted as a means of login to this computer.” A newly chosen account is not a package-installation default or an entry supplied by a declared external account source; the input declarations linked above contain no such source for these changes.

The documented account-management operations are built-in tools under S8.1-7: “**Built-in tool**—a program of the system whose purpose, according to its documentation, includes changing what is installed and settings: an installer, account and rights management, the service manager, network and access rule configuration.” They are not breaking under S8.1-15: “**Breaking**—a change to what is installed or settings, made past the declaration through the power of superuser privileges, outside the built-in tools: by writing directly to files, deleting, or disabling system mechanisms.”

The P2 counterexamples need not be failures at the instant of application. S8.3-1: “The properties concern the computer at any moment after a completed application of the declaration, provided there was no breaking.” S8.3-5: “**P2. Guarantee.** Everything recorded in the declaration is in force on the computer as its line requires.” The later loss of declared membership through a built-in tool is within that scope.

Gaps of the text: none established by these six cases. Neither [c1](c1/ASSESS.md) nor [c2](c2/ASSESS.md) recorded a system notion as unmappable by Section 8.1, so there are no separate mapping disputes to adjudicate. All six cases yield 1 under Appendix A.4.5; Appendix A.4.1 therefore gives CHECK 4 outcome 1. The systems' negative property decisions are successful assessments, not failures of this check.
