# Meaning and evidence of instrument statuses

2026-11-23 UTC. `tables/fact-status.tsv` is a rehearsal/preparation record, withheld from the deciding parties. It is not an object verdict. Each fact group retains the requested records in `tables/MISSING.tsv`; statuses describe their availability through the documented collector routes. `recordable` means the complete named record type was exercised within its stated scope, `partial` means only part of the named record types or coverage succeeded, and `not-obtainable` means the requested record is not supplied by the present routes. That last label does not claim universal impossibility, nor absence from every source in existence. A successful query can return sources without emitting the requested event record. No status answers S8.2-7 or any later classification question.

The numerical and query evidence is the unchanged final rehearsal directory cited on every row. Source-level limitations are visible in `check-inputs-v4/supplement-1/step-4-instruments/S4/collector/extra_alma.py`, `check-inputs-v4/supplement-1/step-4-instruments/S4/collector/extra_void.py`, `check-inputs-v4/supplement-1/step-4-instruments/S4/collector/extra_microos.py`, `check-inputs-v4/supplement-1/step-4-instruments/S4/collector/extra_talos.py` and the inherited `main.py`/`packages.py`. The certificate groups V09/M14 are newly listed, not newly queried: the existing source selectors and emitted schemas have no per-trust-value choice history or complete consumer/use table. Their record status is therefore not-obtainable **with this instrument**, not three failed probes or a finding that no suitable record could exist elsewhere. The authorized sources supply no complete retained choice/use record for them; replaying current-file reads cannot create a missing past event record. Their current metadata/hash routes remain documented, without claiming that the full missing fact was collected. No new query or machine run is justified by this documentation correction.

G01 is partial because two prescribed actions failed, even though boot and collection completed. The 18 recordable groups exclude all partial groups. A14/V08/M12/M13 are four of those 18, but their five dependent IDs are outside the fixed matching tables. This count is instrument record availability, not a count of objects eligible for a decision. M04 has eight dependent IDs and M05 seven; their maximum structural match counts are each one. New-inventory keys may lower either count to zero.

The original source-of-reference labels remain literal collector output for schema compatibility. This revision removes sufficiency judgments from the fact-list and status prose, without changing those runtime bytes, a failure condition, or any sentence of the published text. No class or outcome is assigned to an object, and no textual gap is repaired.
